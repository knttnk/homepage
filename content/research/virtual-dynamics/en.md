# Virtual Dynamics: Assisting Operation by "Altering" the Information Shown to Humans and AI

Many machines are difficult to operate. For example, a human learning to operate an aircraft or a construction machinery, or an AI learning to control a bipedal robot, must go through extensive trial and error and lengthy training.

Among the causes of this difficulty, I focus on dynamics. Dynamics is **the law that governs how the object being operated moves**, and it directly affects how difficult the object is to operate. In my research, I proposed a method named **Virtual Dynamics**, which alters the information shown to the human or AI operating the object, making it appear to have dynamics that are easier to operate than its real ones. On a basic machine, this method has been shown to make operation easier in practice. I aim to develop Virtual Dynamics further and apply it to real-world machines.
